## Record contract

Each training record must contain a unique record ID, role/person reference,
learning objective, material version, delivery date, trainer, attendance result,
assessment method/result, state, approver, review date, and evidence URI. A plan
or slide deck cannot be marked `completed`; attendance alone cannot be marked
`verified`.

Personal data is stored only in an approved restricted system. This repository
may retain a pseudonymous record ID and evidence URI, not attendance signatures
or private assessment details.
