# CAPA Register And Management Review

This document provides the structure required by issue #1245. It does not claim that a real CAPA or management review has occurred.

## CAPA Register

| CAPA ID | Type | Source finding | Severity | Owner | Due date | Containment | Root cause | Correction | Corrective action | Effectiveness review | Closure approval | Status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| CAPA-DRILL-001 | tabletop drill | pending approved drill finding | P2 draft | owner pending | pending | pending | pending | pending | pending | pending | pending | planned |

Rules:

- A drill CAPA must be visibly marked as drill evidence and must not replace a real finding.
- P0 CAPA cannot be closed without containment, root cause, correction, corrective action, effectiveness review, and accountable approval.
- Overdue P0 CAPA count must remain visible in the management review pack.
- Severity downgrades require rationale and approver; silent downgrade is prohibited.

## Minimum Review Outcome

The management review may conclude only one of:

- `readiness exercise incomplete`
- `ready for independent gap assessment`
- `ready for controlled implementation planning`
- `not ready`

It must not state certified, compliant, conformant, or effective unless separately approved by the required reviewers with commit-bound evidence.
